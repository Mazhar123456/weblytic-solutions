Common questions about pricing

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Most projects are split into two or three milestone payments — an upfront deposit, a mid-project payment, and a final payment on delivery.

What happens after my free support period ends?

You can continue with one of our ongoing maintenance plans, or reach out for support on an as-needed basis — whichever works best for your business.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with Starter or Professional and expand their website's features as their business grows.

Do prices include hosting and domain costs?

Hosting and domain registration are billed separately at cost, and we're happy to handle setup and management for you.
